Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

Overview

Munjez is an offline-first productivity app available on Windows, Linux, and Android. It includes task management, habit tracking, calendar, notes, whiteboard, focus timer (Pomodoro), stopwatch, countdown, and customizable desktop widgets. We designed it to keep your data local whenever possible.

Data We Collect

We do not require account creation to use Munjez. The app does not collect personal data, usage analytics, or telemetry. No information is sent to our servers.

Local Data Storage

All your data — tasks, habits, calendar events, notes, whiteboard drawings, focus sessions, countdown timers, and app settings — is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database. Nothing is synced to the cloud. You are responsible for managing backups and device security.

Network Connections

Munjez connects to the internet to check for app updates via GitHub and to download additional ambient sounds on demand. No personal data, usage data, or app content is transmitted during these requests. Once sounds are downloaded, the app works fully offline.

Notifications

Munjez may send local notifications for task reminders, habit reminders, and focus timer alerts. These notifications are processed entirely on your device and are never sent to any server.

File Access

Munjez may access your file system only when you explicitly choose to export or import data (e.g., saving a backup). The app does not scan, index, or access any files without your direct action.

Crash Reports and Diagnostics

Munjez does not automatically send crash reports, analytics, or diagnostics. Logs are stored locally on your device for debugging purposes and are never transmitted.

Third-Party Services

Munjez uses GitHub for update checks and release downloads. The app and website may include links to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Children's Privacy

Munjez is suitable for all ages. Since it works offline and collects no personal data, children can use it safely. However, we recommend that children under 8 use the app with parental supervision.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact
